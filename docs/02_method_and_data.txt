METHOD AND DATA

1. GOAL
Obtain a single, comparable series of heat spots in Europe (2000 to today) from two
families of sensors with different resolution: MODIS (1 km) and VIIRS (375 m).

2. AREA
40 countries: EU-27, United Kingdom, Norway, Switzerland, Iceland, Albania, Bosnia,
Kosovo, North Macedonia, Montenegro, Serbia, Ukraine, Moldova and Belarus.
Russia is excluded. Aland is merged into Finland. List in pipeline/config.py.
Bounding box: lon -32 to 45, lat 27 to 72.

3. SOURCES
- NASA FIRMS (MODIS Terra/Aqua and VIIRS S-NPP/NOAA-20/NOAA-21 hotspots).
    * Full years: yearly per-country zips (firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/country/).
    * Current year: area API (NRT) in tiles; needs the FIRMS_MAP_KEY key.
- NASA GIBS: satellite imagery to view the fires.
- GWIS / Copernicus via Our World in Data: annual burned area per country.
- Natural Earth (50m): borders.
Coverage (web/data/meta.json): Terra from 2000-11, Aqua 2002-07, S-NPP 2012-01,
NOAA-20 2018-04, NOAA-21 2026-01. Last data: 2026-11-14.

4. PIPELINE (folder pipeline/)
00_countries.py       Country polygons (Natural Earth) -> data/europa_paises.geojson.
01_download_firms.py  Yearly zips and recent data through the API.
02_clean.py           Common schema, drops low confidence, assigns a country to each
                      hotspot, detects and removes fixed heat sources.
03_harmonize.py       Per-country MODIS->VIIRS factors and validation.
04_aggregate.py       JSON for the web (series, grid, fires, recent hotspots).
05_reported.py        GWIS burned area (-> web/data/reportado.csv).

5. CLEANING
- MODIS: detections with confidence < 30 are dropped.
- VIIRS: only nominal or high confidence.
- Fixed heat sources: ~1 km cells (0.01 degrees) with VIIRS detections on 25 or more
  days spread over 8 or more months of the year (industry, flares, solar plants).
  Agricultural burning repeats but in few months, and is kept.

6. HARMONIZATION
Common unit: "VIIRS S-NPP equivalent hotspots".
- r[country] = S-NPP / (Terra+Aqua) in the 2012-2020 overlap, with a 90% interval by
  bootstrap over years (500 resamples). European fallback factor if the country has
  fewer than 400 MODIS detections in the overlap.
- a = (Terra+Aqua)/Terra: covers the months before Aqua (2000-11 to 2002-06).
- k20 = S-NPP/NOAA-20 and k21 = S-NPP/NOAA-21: calibrate the newer satellites.
- Each month is counted with ONE sensor (S-NPP > NOAA-20 > NOAA-21 > Terra+Aqua > Terra);
  sensors are never summed.
European values obtained (factores.json): r = 3.22 (CI 3.14-3.31); a = 2.11;
k20 = 0.964; k21 = 1.011.

7. VALIDATION
Each year of the overlap (2012-2020) is hidden, VIIRS is estimated from MODIS and
compared with what was observed, per country and for Europe, with three options:
uncorrected, European factor and per-country factor. Mean yearly error in Europe:
~3.4% corrected versus ~69% using MODIS alone without correction. Details in
web/data/validacion.json.

8. FIRES
Hotspots within <= 2 km and <= 2 days are merged into a fire. Fires with >= 15
(corrected) hotspots are kept. All hotspots are stored for the 200 largest fires in
Europe and the 10 largest per country (maximum 3000 per fire, random sample).

9. DATA LOADED BY THE WEB (web/data/)
The web does not load millions of points: per-country series (series.json, diario.json),
~50 km grid (rejilla.json), fires (incendios.json, incendios_focos.json), individual
hotspots for the last 24 months (focos/YYYY-MM.json), factors and validation, fixed
sources (fuentes_fijas.geojson) and burned area (reportado.csv). Total ~20 MB.

10. LIMITATIONS
- Drawn zones: ~50 km cells, approximate.
- A month with no hotspots may be due to clouds.
- GWIS burned area: another satellite measurement, not an official statistic. Official
  tables (EFFIS) can be added in data/raw/reportado_extra.csv with columns
  iso,year,incendios,hectareas_oficiales.
- The factors are per-country averages over 2012-2020.
- Test data (tests/make_synthetic.py) is invented; the web shows a banner if meta.json
  has "sinteticos": true (it is currently false).
